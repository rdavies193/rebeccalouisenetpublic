About

I am an ARC DECRA Fellow and Senior Lecturer at Swinburne University of Technology. My research investigates how galaxy-scale outflows impact star-formation in galaxies over much of the history of the Universe. To achieve this, I lead observing programs on many of the world's best telescopes including NASA's James Webb Space Telescope, ESO's Very Large Telescope, and the WM Keck Observatory. My research excellence has been recognised through many grants and prizes, highlighted below.
